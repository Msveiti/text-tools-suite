What is VerboMetrics?

VerboMetrics is a free suite of writing and text analysis tools that run entirely in your browser. Count words, characters, sentences and paragraphs in real time, measure lexical density and reading time, find every verb, check readability, and compare text versions — no signup, no upload, no data leaving your device.

Frequently asked questions

How does the word counter work?

Paste or type your text into the editor. Words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, reading time and speaking time update instantly as you type. Nothing is uploaded — all analysis runs in your browser.

Is VerboMetrics free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use with no signup, no account, and no usage limits.

Does it count characters with or without spaces?

Both. The "Chars" stat includes spaces; use the no-spaces count for strict platform limits.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time assumes 200 words per minute; speaking time assumes 125 words per minute.
